page=1 block=7670365 (x=3 y=379) width=39.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=EYRCHG+SegoeUI-Bold wordint.min=1 wordint.max=24
Background/Aims: Chromosomal instability is a well-known factor in the progression of 
page=1 block=80888389 (x=3 y=391) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=VERTKY+SegoeUI wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
different types of cancer, including colorectal cancer. Chromosomal instability  results  in 
page=1 block=80888389 (x=3 y=403) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=OKTPRM+SegoeUI wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
severely  rearranged  karyotypes  and aneuploidy.  Tetraploidy  constitutes  an  intermediate 
page=1 block=80888389 (x=3 y=415) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=VERTKY+SegoeUI wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
phase during the polyploidy/aneuploidy cascade  in oncogenesis, and tetraploid cells are 
page=1 block=80888389 (x=3 y=427) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=VERTKY+SegoeUI wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
particularly resistant to chemotherapy. Whether inhibition of the mitotic protein polo-like 
page=1 block=80888389 (x=3 y=439) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=VERTKY+SegoeUI wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
kinase 1 (PLK1) prevents the survival of tetraploid colon cancer cells is unknown. Methods: 
page=1 block=80888389 (x=3 y=451) width=411.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=VERTKY+SegoeUI wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
Diploid and tetraploid cells were transfected with siPLK1 or treated with PLK1 inhibitor Bi2536 
page=1 block=80888389 (x=3 y=463) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=VERTKY+SegoeUI wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
in combination with spindle poison. Cell toxicity was assessed via crystal violet staining and 
page=1 block=80888389 (x=3 y=475) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=VERTKY+SegoeUI wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
clonogenic assay. Flow cytometry assessment analyzed numerous cell apoptotic parameters 
page=1 block=80888389 (x=3 y=487) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=VERTKY+SegoeUI wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
and  cell  cycle  phases.  Synergistic  activity  between  Bi2536  and  paclitaxel,  vincristine  or 
page=1 block=80888389 (x=3 y=499) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=VERTKY+SegoeUI wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
colchicine was calculated using the CompuSyn software. Results: Inhibition or abrogation of 
page=1 block=80888389 (x=3 y=511) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=VERTKY+SegoeUI wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
PLK1 prevented the survival of colon cancer cells, specifically tetraploid cells. The cell death 
page=1 block=80888389 (x=3 y=523) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=OKTPRM+SegoeUI wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
induced by PLK inhibition was due to mitotic slippage, followed by the activation of the intrinsic 
page=1 block=80888389 (x=3 y=535) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=VERTKY+SegoeUI wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
pathway of apoptosis. We further demonstrated that co-treatment of the tetraploid colon 
page=1 block=80888389 (x=3 y=547) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=VERTKY+SegoeUI wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
cancer cells with a PLK1 inhibitor and the microtubule polymerisation inhibitor vincristine or 
page=1 block=80888389 (x=3 y=559) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=VERTKY+SegoeUI wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
colchicine, but not the microtubule depolymerisation inhibitor paclitaxel, provoked a lethal 
page=1 block=80888389 (x=3 y=571) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=VERTKY+SegoeUI wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
synergistic effect. Conclusion: PLK1 inhibition together with microtubule-targeting chemicals, 
page=1 block=80888389 (x=3 y=583) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=VERTKY+SegoeUI wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
serve as a potent therapeutic strategy for targeting tetraploid cancer cells.

Introduction
page=2 block=88932800 (x=23 y=64) width=59.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=EYRCHG+SegoeUI-Bold wordint.min=1 wordint.max=24
Colorectal cancer (CRC) is one of the most commonly diagnosed cancers and a leading 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=23 y=88) width=390.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
cause of death worldwide. CRC represents 10% of all diagnosed tumours in men and 9.2% in 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=100) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
women, and its global incidence is estimated at 1.4 million cases annually, with 700,000 deaths 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=112) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
in 2012. Moreover, the CRC incidence is expected to increase 60% by 2030, to more than 2.2 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=124) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
million new cases and 1.1 million cancer deaths [1, 2]. Identifying an efficient treatment 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=136) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=YXNSBS+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
is thus crucial to reduce the CRC morbidity rates. One of the cancer hallmarks in general, 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=148) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
and CRC in particular, is genomic instability. This phenomenon increases the probability of 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=160) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
oncogenic events and creates a heterogeneous cell population with extensive abilities to 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=172) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
adapt and evolve [3]. Tetraploidy is a state that can lead to aneuploidy and genomic instability 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=184) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
[4-6]. Tetraploidy represents the condition of having twice as many chromosomes compared 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=196) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
to their normal, diploid counterparts [7, 8]. Tetraploidy can arise from endoreplication (DNA 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=208) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
replication without mitosis) [9], endomitosis (karyokinesis without cytokinesis) [10, 11] or 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=220) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
aberrant cell fusion [12]. Tetraploid cells are frequently found in pre-neoplastic lesions of 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=232) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
intestinal cancers [13-15].
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=244) width=117.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
The persistence of a tetraploid population is often correlated with the inactivation of 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=23 y=256) width=390.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
tumour suppressors retinoblastoma 1 (RB1) and tumour protein p53 [5]. Inactivation of 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=268) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
p53 facilitates the tetraploidisation of cell lines [16, 17]. Similarly, mutations that inactivate 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=280) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
the  adenomatous  polyposis  coli  (APC)  tumour  suppressor  gene  initiate  a  majority  of 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=292) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
colorectal cancers and result in tetraploidisation in vitro and in vivo [18]. Tetraploid cells 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=304) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
are intrinsically resistant to genotoxic stress mediated by ionising irradiation or anti-cancer 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=316) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
chemotherapeutic agents, including platinum compounds and topoisomerase inhibitors [19, 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=328) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
20]. Thus, tetraploid cells might contribute to chemotherapeutic failure. The development 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=340) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
of novel strategies to target tetraploid tumour cells can improve the treatment outcome for 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=352) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
CRC patients.
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=364) width=59.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
PLK1 is  a  member of  the polo-like  kinase (PLK) family and  mediates several key 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=23 y=376) width=390.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
functions  throughout  mitosis,  including  bipolar  arrangement  of  the  centrosomes,  the 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=388) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
spindle assembly checkpoint and cytokinesis [21-23]. PLK1 is located in different subcellular 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=400) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
compartments depending on its function. PLK1 is mostly cytoplasmic during the interphase. 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=412) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
During early mitosis, PLK1 is accumulated at the spindle poles, then in the kinetochores for 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=424) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
chromosomal alignment in the metaphase plate and concentrated in the cytokinetic bridge 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=436) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
during cytokinesis [24, 25]. PLK1 expression levels are elevated in a range of different tumor 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=448) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
types, including invasive breast carcinoma, hepatocellular carcinoma and CRC [26]. PLK1 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=460) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
can promote tumorigenesis in a myriad of ways. First, PLK1 can phosphorylate MDM2 or 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=472) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
p53 and abrogate the p53 function. Second, PLK1 can increase the levels of the oncogenic 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=484) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
transcription factor MYC in a positive feedback loop. Third, PLK1 overexpression leads to 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=496) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
degradation of the tumour suppressor repressor element-1 (RE-1) silencing transcription 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=508) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
(REST) in triple negative breast cancer. Fourth, mainly in hepatocellular carcinoma, hepatitis 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=520) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
B can stimulate PLK1 activation, an action that leads to the repression of several transcription 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=532) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
factors, including SUZ12 and ZNF198. Finally, PLK1 can also inhibit the tumour suppressor 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=544) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
phosphatase  and  tensin  homolog  (PTEN)  and  thus  indirectly  activate  the  oncogenic 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=556) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
phosphoinositide 3-kinase (PI3K) pathway [27].
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=568) width=215.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
Here, we report that PLK1 inhibition leads to the selective destruction of tetraploid 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=23 y=580) width=390.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
cancer cells. In addition, we provide a complete characterisation of the pro-apoptotic signal 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=592) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
transduction pathway provoked by PLK1 inhibition and the benefit of using a combination 
page=2 block=80656360 (x=3 y=604) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=YXNSBS+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
treatment with anti-microtubule polymerisation agents.

page=14 block=80642495 (x=3 y=293) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
carcinoma [42, 53].
page=14 block=80642495 (x=3 y=305) width=86.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
Drug combinations are particularly important in the modern era of chemotherapy; 
page=14 block=80642495 (x=23 y=317) width=390.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
they offer the advantage of synergistic antitumor activity at relatively low concentrations 
page=14 block=80642495 (x=3 y=329) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
of each drug and reduce side effects and additive toxicity. Moreover, compound synergy 
page=14 block=80642495 (x=3 y=341) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
might overcome the drug resistance of certain tumours, including cancers that harbour 
page=14 block=80642495 (x=3 y=353) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
chromosomal instability and particularly tetraploidy, which is highly correlated with drug 
page=14 block=80642495 (x=3 y=365) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
resistance and a poor patient prognosis [20, 54-56]. Due to the key role of PLK1 in several 
page=14 block=80642495 (x=3 y=377) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
cell  cycle  processes,  including  centrosome  maturation,  bipolar  spindle  formation  and 
page=14 block=80642495 (x=3 y=389) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
kinetochore-microtubule attachment, we asked whether there is a lethal interaction between 
page=14 block=80642495 (x=3 y=401) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
PLK1 inhibition and microtubule-destabilising drugs to induce apoptosis. There are two 
page=14 block=80642495 (x=3 y=413) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
types of microtubule poisons widely used in chemotherapy: microtubule depolymerisation 
page=14 block=80642495 (x=3 y=425) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
and polymerisation inhibitors [57, 58]. Paclitaxel (commonly named Taxol) is  the most 
page=14 block=80642495 (x=3 y=437) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
utilised  microtubule  depolymerisation inhibitor  in preclinical  and  clinical models  [59]. 
page=14 block=80642495 (x=3 y=449) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
Microtubule polymerisation inhibitors, including vincristine from the Vinca alkaloid group 
page=14 block=80642495 (x=3 y=461) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
[60] and the colchicine from the Colchicine group, are more diverse [61].
page=14 block=80642495 (x=3 y=473) width=323.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
We found that co-treatment with a low dose of the PLK1 inhibitor Bi2536 and paclitaxel 
page=14 block=80642495 (x=23 y=485) width=390.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=YXNSBS+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
did not induce any synergistic lethality in tetraploid colon cancer cells. On the other hand, 
page=14 block=80642495 (x=3 y=497) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
co-treatment of a low dose of the PLK1 inhibitor Bi2536 and a low dose of either vincristine 
page=14 block=80642495 (x=3 y=509) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
or colchicine  induced strong synergistic  toxicity  in tetraploid colon cancer cells. These 
page=14 block=80642495 (x=3 y=521) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
findings are consistent with previous studies that employed vincristine in combination with 
page=14 block=80642495 (x=3 y=533) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=YXNSBS+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
PLK1 inhibitors for the treatment of various solid tumours and haematological malignancies 
page=14 block=80642495 (x=3 y=545) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
[62-67]. Our study is the first report to use colchicine as an effective treatment strategy in 
page=14 block=80642495 (x=3 y=557) width=410.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=YXNSBS+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
combination with PLK1 inhibition.

page=14 block=80621231 (x=23 y=629) width=390.000000 height=7.000000 fontname=QGNRTI+Cambria wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
