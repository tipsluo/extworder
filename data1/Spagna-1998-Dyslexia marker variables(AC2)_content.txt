Title:
Dyslexia Marker Varia
Abstract:
Despite warnings that the field of learning disabilities 
(LD) must address the issue of population heterogeneity, the LD 
research community still lacks operational definitions of specific 
learning disabilities as well as a systematic approach for reporting 
sample characteristics. Recently, however, a definition of dyslexia 
has been proposed that might signal a significant advance. This 
article builds on this definition of dyslexia by: (a) reintroducing 
the concept of marker variables, (b) proposing a strategy for 
developing an updated marker variable system, (c) presenting 
a preliminary working set of dyslexia marker variables, and 
(d) calling for the eventual adoption of this or similar marker 
variable systems to facilitate future research efforts.
