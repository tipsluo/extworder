page=7 block=1754662105 (x=1 y=301) width=145.000000 height=6.000000 fontname=KNBBNK+Gulliver-Bold wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
Understandability depends on the cognitive load experienced by users in using the decision model to classify instances
