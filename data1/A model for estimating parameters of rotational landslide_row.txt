Abstract: A first-order differential equation was developed and proposed as a search tool in the detection and determination of rotational 
page=1 block=1350751778 (x=1 y=201) width=484 height=6 fontname=VUJFOH+MinionPro-Regular wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
landslides from two epochs of light detection and ranging (LiDAR) system data in the form of 3D points. To test the proposed method 
page=1 block=1350751778 (x=1 y=212) width=484 height=6 fontname=VUJFOH+MinionPro-Regular wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
two epochs of LiDAR data were used: one before and one after a rotational landslide occurred. The first epoch of LiDAR data was real, 
page=1 block=1350751778 (x=1 y=223) width=484 height=5 fontname=VUJFOH+MinionPro-Regular wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
while the second epoch of LiDAR data was simulated based on the first epoch to ensure one or more rotational landslides were included. 
page=1 block=1350751778 (x=1 y=233) width=484 height=6 fontname=VUJFOH+MinionPro-Regular wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
From the last returns of LiDAR data of both epochs, two functional surfaces were created. Then elevation differences were obtained for 
page=1 block=1350751778 (x=1 y=244) width=484 height=6 fontname=VUJFOH+MinionPro-Regular wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
identical points in both surfaces. The differenced elevations mainly contain two types of data; one type consists of unchanged elevation 
page=1 block=1350751778 (x=1 y=255) width=484 height=6 fontname=VUJFOH+MinionPro-Regular wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
differences and the other type includes changed elevation differences. The second type may be considered as outliers with respect to the 
page=1 block=1350751778 (x=1 y=266) width=484 height=6 fontname=VUJFOH+MinionPro-Regular wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
former. Next, segmentation was performed using the determined outliers. Finally, segmented data were used to estimate the rotational 
page=1 block=1350751778 (x=1 y=277) width=484 height=5 fontname=VUJFOH+MinionPro-Regular wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
landslide parameters. Using the model, all rotational landslides were detected and their parameters estimated, which were consistent 
page=1 block=1350751778 (x=1 y=287) width=484 height=6 fontname=VUJFOH+MinionPro-Regular wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
with simulation parameters. In conclusion, the developed model is capable of detecting and determining rotational landslides from 3D 
page=1 block=1350751778 (x=1 y=298) width=484 height=6 fontname=VUJFOH+MinionPro-Regular wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
data.
