page=9 block=-1246462311 (x=116 y=368) width=401.000000 height=6.000000 fontname=MinionPro-Regular wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
particles. If similar phenomena are observed in serious autoimmune- or neuro-degenerative diseases, the current 
page=9 block=-1246462311 (x=116 y=378) width=400.000000 height=6.000000 fontname=MinionPro-Regular wordint.min=2 wordint.max=4
