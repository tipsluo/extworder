4. DISCUSSION AND CONCLUSIONS
page=7 block=611289995 (x=1 y=283) width=175 height=6 fontname=JLFHML+AdvOT114894c7.B wordint.min=1 wordint.max=4
Macroscopic B0 magnetic field inhomogeneity adversely
page=7 block=663466110 (x=13 y=301) width=232 height=6 fontname=JLFHMP+AdvTTa966caec wordint.min=1 wordint.max=5
affects MRI images, especially the ones based on a gradient
page=7 block=663466110 (x=1 y=313) width=244 height=6 fontname=JLFHMP+AdvTTa966caec wordint.min=1 wordint.max=6
echo acquisition. They not only lead to image distortions,10
page=7 block=663466110 (x=1 y=323) width=244 height=8 fontname=JLFHMP+AdvTTa966caec wordint.min=1 wordint.max=5
but also degrade quantitative measurements of relaxation
page=7 block=663466110 (x=1 y=337) width=244 height=6 fontname=JLFHMP+AdvTTa966caec wordint.min=1 wordint.max=7
parameters8,11–15 if not accounted for. Numerous methods
page=7 block=663466110 (x=1 y=346) width=244 height=9 fontname=JLFHMP+AdvTTa966caec wordint.min=1 wordint.max=7
have been proposed to correct for these adverse effects,
page=7 block=663466110 (x=1 y=361) width=244 height=6 fontname=JLFHMP+AdvTTa966caec wordint.min=1 wordint.max=5
including correcting methods during data collection.16–24 and
page=7 block=663466110 (x=1 y=370) width=244 height=8 fontname=JLFHMP+AdvTTa966caec wordint.min=1 wordint.max=7
post processing approaches.25–30 In this paper we focus on
page=7 block=663466110 (x=1 y=382) width=244 height=8 fontname=JLFHMP+AdvTTa966caec wordint.min=1 wordint.max=5
the VSF method1 that allows accounting for the effects of
page=7 block=663466110 (x=1 y=394) width=244 height=8 fontname=JLFHMP+AdvTTa966caec wordint.min=1 wordint.max=6
magnetic field inhomogeneity in data obtained with mGRE
page=7 block=663466110 (x=1 y=409) width=244 height=5 fontname=JLFHMP+AdvTTa966caec wordint.min=1 wordint.max=6
sequences.
page=7 block=663466110 (x=1 y=421) width=42 height=5 fontname=JLFHMP+AdvTTa966caec wordint.min=1 wordint.max=21
Quantitative parameters that are derived using mGRE-
page=7 block=663466110 (x=13 y=433) width=232 height=5 fontname=JLFHMP+AdvTTa966caec wordint.min=1 wordint.max=5
based techniques, such as R2* mapping, are usually suffered
page=7 block=663466110 (x=1 y=445) width=244 height=5 fontname=JLFHMP+AdvTTa966caec wordint.min=1 wordint.max=5
from magnetic field inhomogeneity artifacts. These artifacts
page=7 block=663466110 (x=1 y=457) width=244 height=5 fontname=JLFHMP+AdvTTa966caec wordint.min=1 wordint.max=6
are extremely severe in those regions that are close to the
page=7 block=663466110 (x=1 y=469) width=244 height=5 fontname=JLFHMP+AdvTTa966caec wordint.min=1 wordint.max=7
tissue-air interfaces, i.e. in sinuses. These artifacts often dete-
page=7 block=663466110 (x=1 y=481) width=244 height=5 fontname=JLFHMP+AdvTTa966caec wordint.min=1 wordint.max=5
riorate the quantitative measurements, thus hinder the clinical
page=7 block=663466110 (x=1 y=493) width=244 height=5 fontname=JLFHMP+AdvTTa966caec wordint.min=1 wordint.max=5
application of these quantitative techniques. The VSF method
page=7 block=663466110 (x=1 y=504) width=244 height=6 fontname=JLFHMP+AdvTTa966caec wordint.min=1 wordint.max=6
was proposed previously to correct for this effect, however,
page=7 block=663466110 (x=1 y=516) width=244 height=6 fontname=JLFHMP+AdvTTa966caec wordint.min=1 wordint.max=6
direct voxel-by-voxel calculations of the F-function requires
page=7 block=663466110 (x=1 y=528) width=244 height=6 fontname=JLFHMP+AdvTTa966caec wordint.min=1 wordint.max=6
long computational time. In this study, we presented a
page=7 block=663466110 (x=1 y=540) width=244 height=6 fontname=JLFHMP+AdvTTa966caec wordint.min=1 wordint.max=8
library-driven approach for fast VSF implementation. By
page=7 block=663466110 (x=1 y=552) width=244 height=6 fontname=JLFHMP+AdvTTa966caec wordint.min=1 wordint.max=8
using a pre-calculated library, the calculation time of the F-
page=7 block=663466110 (x=1 y=564) width=244 height=6 fontname=JLFHMP+AdvTTa966caec wordint.min=1 wordint.max=6
function has been reduced from 5 hrs and 30 min to 3 min
page=7 block=663466110 (x=1 y=576) width=244 height=6 fontname=JLFHMP+AdvTTa966caec wordint.min=1 wordint.max=6
on a computer with a standard configuration (3.2 GHz CPU
page=7 block=663466110 (x=1 y=588) width=244 height=6 fontname=JLFHMP+AdvTTa966caec wordint.min=1 wordint.max=5
and 16 GB RAM). By comparing directly with the voxel-
